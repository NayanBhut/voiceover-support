Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

VoiceOver processes text and generates speech on your device using Apple's speech framework. No account is required. The app contains no advertising, tracking, analytics, or cloud text-to-speech service.

Text you type, imported document text, recent conversions, playlists, pronunciation rules, favorite voices, and preferences are stored locally. The app and its widget share a playback snapshot and playback commands through an App Group on your device. The snapshot includes a short title derived from your text, the selected voice, and playback progress. This title can appear in a widget you add to your Home Screen.

Clipboard text is read when you choose Paste. Documents are read when you select them through the file picker. VoiceOver does not request microphone, contacts, location, or photo-library access.

Audio exports are created in temporary app storage. You choose whether to save or share an export and which destination receives it. The selected destination's privacy practices then apply. Downloading additional system voices is managed by Apple.

Clear Recent Conversions removes the recent list. Text, playlists, pronunciation rules, and other saved items remain until you clear them separately or delete the app. Copies you exported or shared remain at their destinations. Apple device backups and system diagnostics are controlled by your device and Apple account settings.

The developer does not receive your text, documents, audio, or usage history from this app. If you contact support, you choose what to send; avoid including private document content. Public support reports can be seen by other visitors.

This notice will be updated when the app's data practices change.
