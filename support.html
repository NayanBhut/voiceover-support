How can we help?

Help with VoiceOver for iPhone and iPad.

Read text aloud

Type or paste text in the Player, choose a voice, and press Play. Switch to the reader to follow word highlighting. Speed and pitch can be adjusted before playback.

Import a document

Choose Import Document and select a file. Text, RTF, and text-based PDF documents are supported. Scanned PDFs need text recognition in another app first. If a Word document cannot be read, save it as text or PDF and import that copy.

Download a different voice

The app shows system voices available on your device. Manage additional spoken-content voices in iPhone or iPad Settings. Downloads may require an internet connection and storage space.

Export and save audio

Open Library → Export Audio, choose WAV, M4A, or AIFF, then export. Use Share Audio File to save to Files or send to another app. The voice determines the generated sample rate.

Manage saved text

Clear Recent Conversions in Settings removes the recent list. Clear text and other saved items separately. Removing the app removes its local app data; exports saved elsewhere are unaffected.

Playback widget

Add the VoiceOver playback widget to your Home Screen. Open the app and load text before using its playback controls. A short title derived from your text may be visible on the widget.

Report a problem or ask a question

Contact support on GitHub

A GitHub account is needed to post. Reports are public. Include your device model, OS version, app version, and the steps that reproduce the problem. Do not post private text, personal documents, passwords, or other sensitive information.
